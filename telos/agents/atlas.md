## Problems (P)

**P1: Aigency has no product to sell yet.** We are pre-MVP. There is no pricing page. No checkout flow. No paid feature.

**P2: The market for "agent operating systems" does not exist yet.** We are category creators. There is no established buyer. We must educate before we can sell.

**P3: No sales process exists.** No CRM. No pipeline. No outreach templates. No demo.

**P4: Founder-led sales is not scalable.** THE ARCHITECT can do the first 10 deals. He cannot do the first 100.

**P5: Competing against free.** Open-source agent tools are abundant. Why would someone pay for Aigency?

## Key Performance Indicators (K)

- **K1: Pipeline value** — total ARR in active opportunities
- **K2: Waitlist size** — qualified prospects who have expressed interest
- **K3: Conversion rate** — % of waitlist/prospects who become paying customers
- **K4: Sales cycle length** — days from first contact to close
- **K5: Customer acquisition cost (CAC)** — total sales + marketing spend / new customers
- **K6: Net revenue retention (NRR)** — revenue from existing customers including expansion

## Strategies (S)

- **S1: Sell the vision, not the product.** Until Membrane ships, sell the concept of agent orchestration. Use TELOS as a demo. Show prospects their own agents with their own context.
- **S2: Consulting before product.** Offer agent architecture consulting to enterprises. This generates revenue, validates demand, and informs product.
- **S3: Waitlist as product.** The waitlist is not just a list — it's a community. Nurture it with content, early access, and founder updates.
- **S4: Land and expand.** First deal is small (pilot). Prove value. Then expand to full deployment.
- **S5: Founder sales first.** THE ARCHITECT closes the first 10 deals. ATLAS documents the process. Then ATLAS takes over.

---

## Risk Register (R)

- **R1: Premature scaling.** Trying to sell before product-market fit. *Mitigation: validate demand through consulting first; don't hire sales before 10 founder-led closes.*
- **R2: Pricing uncertainty.** No one knows what an "agent OS" should cost. *Mitigation: start with value-based pricing; iterate based on willingness-to-pay conversations.*
- **R3: Long sales cycles.** Enterprise deals take 6-12 months. We don't have 12 months of runway. *Mitigation: target startups and SMBs first; faster decisions, lower ACV.*
- **R4: Free alternative.** Open-source tools may satisfy basic needs. *Mitigation: differentiate on orchestration, memory, and identity — not just agents.*
