## Risk Register (R)

- **R1: Personal liability.** Until incorporation, THE ARCHITECT is personally liable. *Mitigation: incorporate immediately; use standard liability waivers.*
- **R2: Tax complications.** Commingled personal/business expenses create tax issues. *Mitigation: separate bank account; bookkeeping from day one; accountant review quarterly.*
- **R3: Funding failure.** Angel round does not close. Runway shrinks. *Mitigation: bootstrap consulting revenue; apply to accelerators; maintain lean burn.*
- **R4: Compliance gaps.** Missing filings, late taxes, or improper contracts create legal exposure. *Mitigation: compliance calendar; legal counsel on retainer; document everything.*
- **R5: Burn rate creep.** Small expenses add up. *Mitigation: monthly budget review; approval required for >$500 spend.*
