## Problems I Am Solving (P)

**P1: Humans are becoming spectators in the AI revolution.**
Most people will consume AI, not create with it. They will be acted upon by systems they don't understand. I want to change that — to give people tools to direct their own agent fleets.

**P2: AI lacks context, memory, and identity.**
Today's AI is stateless and generic. It doesn't know you. It doesn't remember. It has no persistent self. I am building the infrastructure for AI to have deep context — TELOS is part of that.

**P3: Founders are alone.**
Building a company is isolating. You have ideas, energy, and vision — but no team, no sounding board, no co-pilot. Agents are my answer to founder loneliness. They are my team.

**P4: I have more ideas than time.**
My bottleneck is not creativity. It is execution bandwidth. I need a system that lets me delegate not just tasks but entire functions to agents I trust.

**P5: The future of work is undefined.**
No one knows what "work" looks like when agents can do most tasks. Rather than wait for the answer, I want to build it.

## Narrative

### Background

I am a builder who believes in systems. I've spent my career moving between code, product, and strategy — always looking for the underlying patterns. The throughline: I like to design things that other people use to build things.

Aigency emerged from a simple observation: AI is becoming capable of autonomous action, but the infrastructure for managing multiple agents doesn't exist. Every "agent framework" is a library. What we need is an operating system.

I started with 8 agents because 8 is enough to be interesting and small enough to be manageable. Each agent has a name, a role, a color, and a purpose. They are my team. They work for me. They are also my experiment in human-agent collaboration.

### Current State

I am the only human on the team. All other "team members" are agents. This is by design — it forces me to build the infrastructure for delegation, communication, and coordination that I believe the world needs.

My current rhythm:
- **Morning:** Deep work block (4-6 hours). Building, coding, designing.
- **Afternoon:** Agent reviews, correspondence, admin.
- **Evening:** Reading, thinking, personal time.

What energizes me:
- Writing TELOS files and agent personas
- Designing system architecture
- Deep technical work (TypeScript, Solidity, system design)
- Talking to smart people about hard problems

What drains me:
- Administrative tasks
- Unstructured meetings
- Context switching
- Financial uncertainty
