## Problems (P)

**P1: Aigency has zero online presence.** No website. No blog. No social media. No one knows we exist.

**P2: Multi-agent systems are hard to explain.** The concept is abstract. Most people don't understand why they need multiple agents instead of one chatbot.

**P3: The AI space is noisy.** Every day a new "AI agent" tool launches. Cutting through the noise requires differentiation and repetition.

**P4: Content without distribution is useless.** Even great content dies if no one sees it. We need channels, audiences, and sharing mechanisms.

**P5: Founder-led marketing is not scalable.** THE ARCHITECT cannot be the only public face. The brand needs its own voice.

## Strategies (S)

- **S1: Category creation.** Don't compete in "AI agents." Create "Agent Operating Systems" as a category. Own the narrative.
- **S2: Founder + agent co-authorship.** THE ARCHITECT writes the deep technical pieces. ECHO writes the accessible summaries, threads, and social posts. Both voices are visible.
- **S3: Show, don't tell.** The best marketing for Aigency is Aigency itself. Publish agent conversations, TELOS files, and squad updates as content.
- **S4: Community before product.** Build an audience of agent-curious developers before the product is ready. Ship content, not just code.
- **S5: Repurpose everything.** One long-form essay becomes: a blog post, a Twitter thread, a LinkedIn post, a newsletter entry, a Discord announcement, and a Hacker News submission.

---

## Risk Register (R)

- **R1: Quality vs. quantity tradeoff.** Two pieces per week is ambitious for a pre-product startup. Low-quality content damages brand. *Mitigation: start with 1/week; increase only if quality holds.*
- **R2: Platform dependency.** X/Twitter algorithm changes. LinkedIn reach drops. *Mitigation: own the channel (newsletter + website) first; social is amplification.*
- **R3: No visual identity.** ECHO needs IRIS's design system to create professional assets. *Mitigation: use minimal, clean design until IRIS delivers; prioritize words over visuals.*
- **R4: Founder time.** THE ARCHITECT must co-create content. If he's heads-down building, content stalls. *Mitigation: ECHO writes first drafts; ARCHITECT edits; 30-min review max.*
